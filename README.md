## 🛠 Featured Projects

### 🛢 1. GAIA Petroleum Intelligence Suite
*An advanced AI suite for the energy sector.*
- **Core Functionality:** Economic and geological reasoning, orbital spectral analysis, and basin verification.
- **Impact:** Automates the complex research required for petroleum exploration and asset valuation.
- **Tech:** Python, Agentic Reasoners, SQLite (Basin Data).

### 🤖 2. Zenith Sentinel: Autonomous Trading
*A high-precision market execution system.*
- **Core Functionality:** Multi-stage trailing stop-losses (3x, 5x, 10x), high-conviction "snipe" execution, and visual market feedback.
- **Impact:** Provides a disciplined, AI-driven approach to volatile markets, minimizing human error and maximizing efficiency.
- **Tech:** Python, Real-time APIs, Financial Modeling.

### 💼 3. Oil & Gas Career Bot
*Professional recruitment automation for the Nigerian energy industry.*
- **Core Functionality:** Researching 16+ top Nigerian energy firms (Seplat, Sahara, Aradel), generating tailored AI-centric cover letters, and managing application logs.
- **Impact:** Bridges the gap between technical petroleum engineering expertise and modern digital transformation.
- **Tech:** Python, LLM-based Content Generation, PDF Automation.

### 📈 4. AlphaSweep (Aether Companion)
*Advanced market indicators for TradingView.*
- **Core Functionality:** Custom Pine Script indicators designed for high-frequency signal identification.
- **Impact:** Visualizes market momentum and supply/demand zones with institutional-grade accuracy.
- **Tech:** Pine Script (TradingView).

### 👟 5. Instagram Shoe Bot (E-commerce)
*E-commerce automation for global retail.*
- **Core Functionality:** Google Veo integration for video generation, content performance optimization loops, and automated posting schedules.
- **Impact:** Streamlines social media marketing for high-end retail businesses.
- **Tech:** Python, Google Veo API, Social Media Automation.
